    Analog and port pins

    Analog names are what the firmware does with each input, found by experiment (see INPUT_MAP.md); the multiplexer is inferred. Port rows show bits 7…0; dashed bits are outputs. Hover a bit for what it is; question marks are inferences. Details in INPUT_MAP.md. F0 is ignition; F5 low means the serial peripheral is ready.

    Serial peripherals (SPI)

    Most recent transfers, newest last. Select 1E and 1D are the two external devices, one of them the co-processor.

    Scripted replies

    One rule per line, hex, first match wins. Anything unmatched keeps the stand-in (two's complement of the previous byte), which startup needs. A rule that breaks that handshake makes the firmware restart forever.
